What you can do

One place for every place you've been.

You save a place only once. Every time you go back, you add a visit with its rating.

The map

Your whole world at a glance.

The map is the app's home screen. Each category has its own colour and icon, and at the top you instantly see how many trips, cities and countries you've collected.

  • Category filters: tap to hide, long-press to show only that one.
  • Search your places, cities and countries.
  • Open a place in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze.
Quick add

At a restaurant? Save it in three taps.

Long-press the category icon and pick a rating and price. GPS finds the location, city and country; the date and your current trip are added automatically. You just type the name: everything is saved.

  • Location, city and country from GPS in one tap.
  • Date and open trip added automatically.
  • Been there before? OYWA reminds you and just adds a new visit.
Stamp ratings

Remember what you really loved.

Restaurants, hotels, bars, ice-cream shops and beaches are rated with a stamp, like in a passport: Poor, Average, Great or Excellent. You can add the price range too.

  • Every visit has a month, year, rating and notes.
  • Link the visit to a trip, for example “Summer in Sicily”.
Countries and badges

Watch your world map fill up.

The countries you've visited are coloured on the world map, with a percentage for each continent. Categories unlock badges with three levels: Stamp, Visa and Passport.

Inside the app

Designed like a travel poster.

Pastel colours, soft shapes and stamps: an archive you'll want to open.

Your data stays yours

Your places. Only yours. Forever.

OYWA has no server collecting your travels. Your places, visits and ratings are stored on your device.

  • Everything on your phone. No account, no login.
  • No ads and no tracking.
  • Back up whenever you like, in a file you keep.

To show the map and search for places the app uses OpenStreetMap. What is sent, and when, is explained in the privacy policy.
