FAQ

Everything you want to know about OYWA.

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Write to us: we reply to everyone. staff@oywa.it

What is OYWA?

An app to save and remember the places you visit when you travel (cities, restaurants, hotels, beaches, monuments) on a personal map, with ratings, trips, visited countries and badges.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start right away: no sign-up, email or password.

Where does my data go?

It stays only on your phone. OYWA has no servers collecting it and nobody else sees it.

How much does it cost?

The test version is free. The price of the final version will be announced at launch, with no subscriptions and no ads.

When is it out? Which phones does it work on?

It will be available for iPhone and Android. The test version is planned by the end of October 2026: you can sign up with the buttons on this site.

How do I join the test version?

Tap “Try on iPhone” or “Try on Android”, type your name and press Send: a ready-made email opens (on a computer you can also use Gmail or Outlook). We'll let you know when the test version is ready and send you the instructions to install it.

Does it work without internet?

Your places and visits are always available. You need a connection to see the map and search for places; offline you can still save a place with its GPS position and enter the city and country by hand.

Can I import places saved on Google Maps?

Yes, from a KML or KMZ file (for example exported from Google My Maps). You can review them and choose the category before saving.

What happens if I change or lose my phone?

In Settings you create a full backup in a file and restore it on your new phone. Since the data is only on your phone, it can't be recovered without a backup: you can turn on a monthly reminder.

Can I share my places with friends?

Yes: select the places and export them to PDF, Excel or KML, or to the OYWA format for people who use the app.

Which languages is it available in?

Italian, English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Can I delete all my data?

Yes, from Settings → Your data → Delete all data. Deletion is permanent.

Want to be among the first to try it?

We're preparing the test version for iPhone and Android. Write to us and we'll let you know as soon as it's ready. You can use the same address for any question.
